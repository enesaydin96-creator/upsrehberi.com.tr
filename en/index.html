Online or line interactive?

The two technologies serve different needs. The right choice depends on the equipment you protect.

Line interactive

Supplies the load from the mains, corrects voltage fluctuations with an automatic regulator and switches to battery during an outage.

Transfer time
A few milliseconds
Waveform on battery
Simulated sine
Power range
650 – 3000 VA
Best for
Home, office, modems, video recorders, gaming
Volt LN Plus series

Online double conversion

Supplies the load from the inverter at all times; the output is independent of the mains. Voltage, frequency and noise problems never reach the load.

Transfer time
0 ms
Waveform
Pure sine in every mode
Power range
1 – 200 kVA
Best for
Servers, medical, laboratories, production
