Power from tradition to the future

Dotvolt was founded in the last quarter of 2021 with 100% Turkish capital, by a team with many years of experience in power electronics.

A solution partner, not just a product supplier

Power continuity is now a precondition for both production and digital infrastructure. Dotvolt designs and supplies the equipment that keeps it running and brings it to the field through its partner network: line interactive, single-phase and three-phase online UPS systems, lead-acid batteries from 7 Ah to 200 Ah, servo and static voltage regulators, diesel and petrol generators, and surge protection sockets rated up to 1050 joules.

Every project starts with a needs analysis. From site survey and battery sizing to commissioning and maintenance, we stay with our customers. Our goal is to bring a fresh approach to power electronics in Türkiye and to become a preferred brand internationally.
