Volt LN Plus

650 – 3000 VA Line Interactive UPS

Line interactive UPS with automatic voltage regulation and LED-LCD panel for modems, computers, video recorders and small office equipment.

Phase (input/output)1/1
Range650 VA – 3 kVA

  • Cold start: power on from battery without mains

  • Short circuit, overload and deep discharge protection
  • Battery charging when switched off
