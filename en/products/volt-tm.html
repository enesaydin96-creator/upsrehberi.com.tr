Volt TM

10 – 20 kVA Online UPS · 3-phase in / 1-phase out

Transformerless high-frequency online UPS fed from a three-phase supply to protect single-phase loads.

TechnologyOnline double conversion
Phase (input/output)3/1
Range10 kVA – 20 kVA
  • 3/1 phase (also selectable as 1/1)
  • Input power factor ≥ 0.95
  • Output power factor 0.9
  • Input range 190–520 VAC
  • N+X parallel operation (optional)
  • Cold start
